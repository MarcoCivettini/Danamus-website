# Proposal

## Why

The user's feedback on the preview of `landing-visual-rhythm`:

- **The timeline compression in "Venti minuti di video, cinque di gioco" is not clear.** The pauses shrink in step with a short scroll, nothing shows what was removed, and the numbers have no source.
- **The pointer glow should not stay in the phone-and-computer section.** The user wants it as a green glow on the background of the whole site.
- **The hero phone is a passive recording.** The user wants a hands-on demo of the editor: a fake app that plays a real match, shows the valid segments in green, and lets the visitor pause, scrub and move the playhead.

There is a real curated project on the Mac: "KIM KUM YONG vs CHRISTINA KALLBERG", with 19 rallies cut by hand by the user. Play is 104.5 s out of 427 s of video, so 24%: the same ratio as "20 minutes, 5 of play". The demo and the timeline can therefore show real data, not invented data.

## What Changes

- **Interactive editor demo in the hero** (**BREAKING** for the hero: the preview recording `clip-preview` is replaced). Inside the phone frame a fake editor, drawn in HTML, mimics the app's mobile editor:
  - a video excerpt of the real match;
  - a timeline with the project's green segments and a playhead;
  - play/pause, a seek bar and the counter "Segmento N di M".

  It autoplays muted (with motion allowed). The visitor can pause, drag the timeline or the playhead and seek. Export, editing the intervals and every other control are visible but inert, and none of them leaves the page.
- **Timeline animation rewritten.** Instead of the scroll scrub there is a sequence that plays once when the section comes into view:
  1. a playhead runs along the bar;
  2. the pauses go out and disappear, and the rallies close up;
  3. the outline of the original length stays visible, with "−N min di pause";
  4. the counter drops to the play duration.

  The bar uses the 19 real rallies of the project, with a caption naming the match. There is a "Rivedi" button to replay it. With reduced motion or without JS: the two static bars, as now.
- **Green glow following the pointer, across the whole site.** A blurred green glow behind the content, on every page, only on devices with a hovering pointer and with motion allowed. It replaces the local spotlight of the phone-and-computer section. The bands become slightly translucent so the glow shows through them too.
- **Media from the curated project.** A script reads the project's intervals from the app's database (read-only) and writes them to a data file in the repo. It also cuts and encodes the video excerpt for the demo from the original file, with ffmpeg from the app bundle. The footage is tracked in the manifest like the other media.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `hero-editor-demo`: the interactive fake editor in the hero. Contents, what the visitor can and cannot do, autoplay, accessibility, and static behaviour without JS or motion.

### Modified Capabilities
- `landing-page`:
  - the hero shows the editor demo instead of a recording;
  - the problem timeline plays a sequence on entry with real data, not a scroll scrub.
- `site-visual-identity`:
  - the pointer glow moves from the device cards to the background of the whole site;
  - the timeline motion is described as a one-off sequence on entry.
- `product-media`:
  - the hero needs the demo excerpt and its segments instead of `clip-preview`;
  - the media derived from the curated project are generated by a script and tracked.

## Impact

- **Precondition:** archive `landing-visual-rhythm` first (this change modifies its timeline and media requirements).
- **New code:**
  - `src/components/EditorDemo.astro` with its own script, lazy-loaded after LCP;
  - `src/data/demo-project.json` (intervals, duration, match name);
  - `scripts/make-demo-media.mjs`;
  - a global glow script and style.
- **Modified code:**
  - `Hero.astro`, `Timeline.astro` and `Problem.astro`;
  - `Platforms.astro` (local spotlight removed);
  - `src/scripts/motion.ts` (timeline sequence, glow, scrub removed);
  - `global.css` (translucent `.band`, glow layer);
  - `src/data/media.ts` and the README.
- **Media:** a new `public/media/demo-match.mp4`, about 140 s at 640×360, H.264 with no audio, about 2 MB, with its poster. `preview.mp4` stays in the repo but is no longer required. The footage comes from the ITTF/WTT broadcast already used and marked `cleared` in the manifest. The excerpt is longer (140 s compared with the 2 minutes recorded before), so the user keeps responsibility for the rights.
- **Performance:**
  - the demo video loads only after the page has loaded, and the LCP stays on the hero poster;
  - the glow is updated with `requestAnimationFrame` and runs only on a hovering pointer.
- **Dependencies:** none new.
